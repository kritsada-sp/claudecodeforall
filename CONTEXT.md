# Thai Food Recommendation Site

A single-page site that recommends Thai dishes: it names one dish as today's pick and lets the visitor mark dishes they like.

## Language

**Dish**:
One entry in the site's menu list (e.g. Pad Thai, Tom Yum Goong). Identified by name.
_Avoid_: Menu item, food, recipe

**Dish of the Day**:
The single dish featured for today: chosen automatically the first time the page is opened each day, or re-chosen on demand by pressing the reroll button. Once chosen, it stays the same for the rest of the calendar day; a new day clears it and triggers a fresh automatic pick. A reroll never picks the Dish that was just displaced — it always names a different Dish.
_Avoid_: Random pick, today's suggestion, featured dish

**Favorites**:
The set of dishes a visitor has individually marked via a toggle on each dish's card. A visitor may favorite any number of dishes at once, independently of the Dish of the Day.
_Avoid_: Liked dishes, saved dishes, bookmarks
